HIGH FREQUENCY TOPICS CBSE Class 10 Board Essentials

Must-Practice & Repeated Questions 🎯

Har subject ke sabse zyaada repeat hone wale topics aur 5-star expected questions. Har mastered topic par +50 Bonus XP milenge!

Chemical Reactions & Equations

5-7 Marks
  • Balancing Chemical Equations: Direct 2-3 mark questions every year.
  • Types of Reactions: Combination, Decomposition (Thermal & Electrolytic), Displacement, Redox.
  • Repeated PYQ: Why does copper sulphate solution color change when an iron nail is dipped in it?

Light - Reflection & Refraction

7-8 Marks
  • Ray Diagrams: Concave Mirror (Object at C, F) & Convex Lens (Virtual image case).
  • Numericals: Lens Formula ($1/f = 1/v - 1/u$) and Mirror Formula with Sign Convention.
  • Repeated PYQ: Power of Lens calculations and Refractive Index based numericals.

Life Processes (Biology)

8-9 Marks
  • Diagrams: Human Digestive System, Structure of Heart, Nephron (Excretion).
  • Aerobic vs Anaerobic Respiration: Breakdown of glucose by various pathways.
  • Repeated PYQ: Double circulation in human heart and role of stomata in transpiration.

Electricity & Magnetic Effects

7-9 Marks
  • Ohm's Law: V-I graph & Resistors in Series/Parallel combinations.
  • Joule's Heating Effect: $H = I^2Rt$ numericals & Electric Power ($P=VI$).
  • Solenoid & Fleming's Left Hand Rule: Magnetic field lines pattern.

Nationalism in India (History)

Must 5 Marks
  • Non-Cooperation Movement vs Civil Disobedience Movement: Difference & why Gandhi withdrew NCM.
  • Salt March / Dandi March: Why Salt was chosen as a symbol of resistance.
  • Map Work: Congress Sessions (Calcutta, Nagpur, Madras) & Satyagraha centers (Champaran, Kheda, Ahmedabad).

Federalism & Power Sharing (Civics)

6-8 Marks
  • Belgium vs Sri Lanka Model: Accommodation policy of Belgium.
  • Decentralization in India (1992 Amendment): Panchayati Raj & Municipality 5 key features.
  • Union, State & Concurrent Lists: Examples and jurisdiction.

Money & Credit + Globalization (Eco)

7-9 Marks
  • Formal vs Informal Sources of Credit: Role of RBI in monitoring banks.
  • Self-Help Groups (SHGs): How SHGs help rural poor and women.
  • MNCs & Production Integration: Joint ventures and local buying.

Agriculture & Resources (Geo)

5-6 Marks
  • Crop Conditions: Rice, Wheat, Sugarcane, Tea, Cotton (Temperature, Rainfall, Soil).
  • Types of Farming: Primitive Subsistence, Intensive Subsistence, Commercial.
  • Map Work: Major Dams (Salal, Bhakra Nangal, Hirakud, Tehri, Rana Pratap Sagar).

Triangles & Circles (Geometry)

10-12 Marks
  • Basic Proportionality Theorem (Thales Theorem): Proof & Direct Questions.
  • Tangents to a Circle: Theorem - Tangents drawn from external point are equal.
  • Repeated Question: Proofs involving cyclic quadrilaterals and circumscribed circles.

Trigonometry & Applications

10-12 Marks
  • Trigonometric Identities: Proving trigonometric equations using $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$.
  • Heights & Distances: 5-mark word problems with angles of elevation and depression ($30^\circ, 45^\circ, 60^\circ$).

Real Numbers & Polynomials

6 Marks
  • Irrationality Proof: Prove that $\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, 5-\sqrt{3}$ is irrational. (Guaranteed 3-mark question).
  • Zeros & Coefficients: Quadratic polynomial zeros finding and relationship verification.

Statistics & Surface Areas

10 Marks
  • Mean, Median & Mode: Finding missing frequencies ($f_1, f_2$) using Ogive / Empirical formula.
  • Combination of Solids: Cone on Hemisphere, Cylinder with Hemispherical ends volume and area.
